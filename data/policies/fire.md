# Fire

## Covered

- Damage to the building and contents caused by fire, lightning, explosion, and smoke from a sudden fire.

## Not covered

- Scorch or heat damage without an actual fire.
- Damage from deliberate acts by the policyholder.

## Documentation

- The fire service report.
- An assessor visit for all fire claims above 50,000 DKK. The assessor's report is required before a decision.

## Deductible

The standard deductible applies.
