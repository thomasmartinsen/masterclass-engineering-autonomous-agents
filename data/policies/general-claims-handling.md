# General claims handling

## Reporting

- A claim must be reported as soon as possible and no later than 12 months after the incident date.
- Claims reported later than 12 months after the incident are rejected unless the customer can document why reporting was not possible.

## Required information

Before a claim can move to `UnderReview`, the case must contain:

1. The incident date.
2. A description that explains what happened, where, and what was damaged or lost.
3. The claimed amount and currency.
4. Category-specific documentation, as described in the category policy.

If information is missing, set the case to `AwaitingInformation` and tell the customer exactly what is missing.

## Decisions

- Only a human case handler may approve or reject a claim. Automated systems may propose a decision, with evidence, but must not set the status to `Approved` or `Rejected` themselves.
- Claims with a claimed amount above 100,000 DKK must be escalated to a senior case handler.
- Every decision must reference the policy sections it is based on.

## Deductible

The standard deductible is 2,500 DKK per claim unless a category policy states otherwise.
