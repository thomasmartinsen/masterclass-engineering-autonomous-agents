# Travel delay

## Covered

Compensation when the departure of a booked flight, train, or ferry is delayed for reasons outside the traveller's control:

| Delay | Compensation |
| --- | --- |
| 4 to 8 hours | Documented expenses for meals and transport, up to 1,500 DKK per traveller |
| More than 8 hours | Documented expenses, up to 3,000 DKK per traveller, including one hotel night |

## Documentation

- Confirmation of the delay from the carrier.
- Receipts for the expenses claimed.

## Not covered

- Delays the carrier already compensates under EU261 or similar rules, up to the amount already paid.
- Delays caused by the traveller arriving late for check-in.

## Deductible

No deductible applies to travel delay compensation.
