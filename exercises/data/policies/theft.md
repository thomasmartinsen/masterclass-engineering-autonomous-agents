# Theft

## Covered

- Theft from the home after forced entry.
- Theft from a locked vehicle where forced entry is visible. Maximum 20,000 DKK per incident.
- Theft of bicycles that were locked. Maximum 15,000 DKK per bicycle.

## Documentation

- A police report number. The theft must be reported to the police within 24 hours of discovery.
- Receipts, or other proof of ownership and value, for each stolen item.
- For bicycles: the frame number.

## Not covered

- Theft of items left in an unlocked vehicle or an unlocked home.
- Cash above 2,000 DKK.

## Deductible

The standard deductible applies.
