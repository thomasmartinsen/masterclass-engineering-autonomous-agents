# Water damage

## Covered

- Sudden and accidental escape of water from pipes, radiators, water heaters, and fixed installations.
- Resulting damage to floors, walls, fixed cabinets, and contents.

## Not covered

- Gradual leaks, seepage, damp, or mould that developed over time.
- Damage caused by lack of maintenance or known defects that were not repaired.
- The cost of repairing the pipe or installation itself.

## Documentation

- Photos of the damage.
- Invoice or statement from the plumber or tradesperson who repaired the leak.
- Receipts or estimates for damaged items above 5,000 DKK.

## Deductible

The standard deductible applies.
